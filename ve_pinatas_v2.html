Piñata Prices

We make customizable piñatas! Show us a photo, tell us your ideas, give us the theme and we will create it for you!

  • Small (2ft–2½ ft) $25–$35
  • Medium (3ft) $35–$45
  • Large (4ft) $50–$60
  • XL (5ft) $70–$80

These prices are not set and will vary depending on what the buyer wants. If you have a special request for a piñata please ask us and we can create an estimate! Thank you!

Production Timeline

Every piñata is handmade from start to finish, so please plan ahead! Custom orders typically take 2–3 weeks to complete. We work on one order at a time, so a new order won't begin until the current piñata is finished. We recommend reaching out as early as possible to make sure your piñata is ready in time for your event.
